Ball economy

A tier-T ball deals DMG_BASE · (MERGE_REQUIRED · MERGE_DAMAGE_MULT)^(T−1). With the defaults that is 10 → 300 → 9000 …: a merged ball is worth 3× the ten balls it consumed, so merging always pays off. Ball price climbs with the total ever bought.
