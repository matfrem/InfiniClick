A universe spans ? levels (your current grid), so the difficulty multiplier from one universe to the next is COST_GROWTH ^ blocks = ?×. That is where the old hard-coded “×10 per universe” now comes from — tune COST_GROWTH (or the grid size) to move it.

Board cost vs reward vs ball power, per level

“Ball power” is the total damage of every ball you own if you spend every shard as soon as you have it (buy tier-1 balls, merge whenever you hit ten). Where it dips under the board cost is where the balls start to struggle and the level slows down.

Time per level (simulated)

From simul.html: N balls clear an empty board in a measured time (geometry), and a board that out-HPs your damage takes cost / (hits·s⁻¹ · total damage) — whichever is slower wins. Uses the greedy “buy everything, merge ASAP” loadout above. Re-run simul.html if you change the grid.
