InfiniClick — Simulator

Runs the real ball/brick physics on the shared layout (layout.js, a normalised square field) to measure two purely geometric quantities: how many brick-hits one ball lands per second, and how long N balls take to sweep a whole board clean. Because the field is resolution-independent, these numbers hold on every device. They feed the time-per-level model in stats.html.
